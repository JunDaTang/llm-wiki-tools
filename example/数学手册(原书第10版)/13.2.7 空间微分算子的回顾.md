#### 13.2.7 空间微分算子的回顾

13.2.7.1 空间微分算子的运算法则

$U , U _ { 1 } , U _ { 2 }$ 和 是标量函数； 是常数 $\vec { V } , \vec { V } _ { 1 } , \vec { V } _ { 2 }$ 是向量函数：

$$
\operatorname{grad} \left(U _ {1} + U _ {2}\right) = \operatorname{grad} U _ {1} + \operatorname{grad} U _ {2}.\tag{13.84}
$$

$$
\operatorname{grad} (c U) = c \operatorname{grad} U.\tag{13.85}
$$

$$
\operatorname{grad} \left(U _ {1} U _ {2}\right) = U _ {1} \operatorname{grad} U _ {2} + U _ {2} \operatorname{grad} U _ {1}.\tag{13.86}
$$

$$
\operatorname{grad} F (U) = F ^ {\prime} (U) \operatorname{grad} U.\tag{13.87}
$$

$$
\operatorname{div} \left(\vec {V} _ {1} + \vec {V} _ {2}\right) = \operatorname{div} \vec {V} _ {1} + \operatorname{div} \vec {V} _ {2}.\tag{13.88}
$$

$$
\operatorname{div} (c \vec {V}) = c \operatorname{div} \vec {V}.\tag{13.89}
$$

$$
\operatorname{div} (U \vec {V}) = \vec {V} \cdot \operatorname{grad} U + U \operatorname{div} \vec {V}.\tag{13.90}
$$

$$
\mathrm{rot} (\vec {V} _ {1} + \vec {V} _ {2}) = \mathrm{rot} \vec {V} _ {1} + \mathrm{rot} \vec {V} _ {2}.\tag{13.91}
$$

$$
\operatorname{rot} \left(c \vec {V}\right) = c \operatorname{rot} \vec {V}.\tag{13.92}
$$

$$
\operatorname{rot} \left(U \vec {V}\right) = U \operatorname{rot} \vec {V} - \vec {V} \times \operatorname{grad} U.\tag{13.93}
$$

$$
\operatorname{div} \operatorname{rot} \vec {V} \equiv 0.\tag{13.94}
$$

$$
\operatorname{rot} \operatorname{grad} U \equiv {\vec {0}} (\text {零向量}).\tag{13.95}
$$

$$
\operatorname{div} \operatorname{grad} U = \Delta U.\tag{13.96}
$$

$$
\mathrm{rot} \mathrm{rot} \vec {V} = \mathrm{grad} \mathrm{div} \vec {V} - \Delta \vec {V}.\tag{13.97}
$$

$$
\operatorname{div} \left(\vec {V} _ {1} \times \vec {V} _ {2}\right) = \vec {V} _ {2} \cdot \operatorname{rot} \vec {V} _ {1} - \vec {V} _ {1} \cdot \operatorname{rot} \vec {V} _ {2}.\tag{13.98}
$$
