#### 6.2.3 多元函数的微分法则

6.2.3.1 复合函数的微分

1. 一元复合函数

$$
u = f (x _ {1}, x _ {2}, \dots , x _ {n}), x _ {1} = x _ {1} (\xi), x _ {2} = x _ {2} (\xi), \dots , x _ {n} = x _ {n} (\xi),\tag{6.51a}
$$

$$
\frac {\partial u}{\partial \xi} = \frac {\partial u}{\partial x _ {1}} \frac {\mathrm{d} x _ {1}}{\mathrm{d} \xi} + \frac {\partial u}{\partial x _ {2}} \frac {\mathrm{d} x _ {2}}{\mathrm{d} \xi} + \dots + \frac {\partial u}{\partial x _ {n}} \frac {\mathrm{d} x _ {n}}{\mathrm{d} \xi}.\tag{6.51b}
$$

2. 多元复合函数

(6.52a)

$$
\left. \begin{array}{c} u = f (x _ {1}, x _ {2}, \dots , x _ {n}), \\ x _ {1} = x _ {1} (\xi , \eta , \dots , \tau), x _ {2} = x _ {2} (\xi , \eta , \dots , \tau), \dots , x _ {n} = x _ {n} (\xi , \eta , \dots , \tau), \\ \left. \begin{array}{c} \frac {\partial u}{\partial \xi} = \frac {\partial u}{\partial x _ {1}} \frac {\partial x _ {1}}{\partial \xi} + \frac {\partial u}{\partial x _ {2}} \frac {\partial x _ {2}}{\partial \xi} + \dots + \frac {\partial u}{\partial x _ {n}} \frac {\partial x _ {n}}{\partial \xi}, \\ \frac {\partial u}{\partial \eta} = \frac {\partial u}{\partial x _ {1}} \frac {\partial x _ {1}}{\partial \eta} + \frac {\partial u}{\partial x _ {2}} \frac {\partial x _ {2}}{\partial \eta} + \dots + \frac {\partial u}{\partial x _ {n}} \frac {\partial x _ {n}}{\partial \eta}, \\ \dots \dots \\ \frac {\partial u}{\partial \tau} = \frac {\partial u}{\partial x _ {1}} \frac {\partial x _ {1}}{\partial \tau} + \frac {\partial u}{\partial x _ {2}} \frac {\partial x _ {2}}{\partial \tau} + \dots + \frac {\partial u}{\partial x _ {n}} \frac {\partial x _ {n}}{\partial \tau}. \end{array} \right\} \\ \end{array} \right.\tag{6.52b}
$$

6.2.3.2 隐函数的微分

(1) 若一元函数 $y = f \left( x \right)$ 由方程

$$
F (x, y) = 0\tag{6.53a}
$$

给出，则利用 (6.51b) 可对 (6.53a) 进行微分

$$
F _ {x} + F _ {y} y ^ {\prime} = 0,\tag{6.53b}
$$

故

$$
y ^ {\prime} = - \frac {F _ {x}}{F _ {y}} (F _ {y} \neq 0).\tag{6.53c}
$$

用同样的方法可得 (6.53b) 的微分

$$
F _ {x x} + 2 F _ {x y} y ^ {\prime} + F _ {y y} (y ^ {\prime}) ^ {2} + F _ {y} y ^ {\prime \prime} = 0.\tag{6.53d}
$$

(6.53b) ，有

$$
y ^ {\prime \prime} = \frac {2 F _ {x} F _ {y} F _ {x y} - (F _ {y}) ^ {2} F _ {x x} - (F _ {x}) ^ {2} F _ {y y}}{(F _ {y}) ^ {3}}.\tag{6.53e}
$$

利用类似的方法可计算三阶导数

$$
F _ {x x x} + 3 F _ {x x y} y ^ {\prime} + 3 F _ {x y y} \left(y ^ {\prime}\right) ^ {2} + F _ {y y y} \left(y ^ {\prime}\right) ^ {3} + 3 F _ {x y} y ^ {\prime \prime} + 3 F _ {y y} y ^ {\prime} y ^ {\prime \prime} + F _ {y} y ^ {\prime \prime \prime} = 0,\tag{6.53f}
$$

巾此可把 $y ^ { \prime \prime \prime }$ 表示出来．

(2) 若多元函数 $u = f \left( x _ { 1 } , x _ { 2 } , \cdot \cdot \cdot , x _ { i } , \cdot \cdot \cdot , x _ { n } \right)$ 由方程

$$
F (x _ {1}, x _ {2}, \dots , x _ {i}, \dots , x _ {n}, u) = 0\tag{6.54a}
$$

给出，可用类似前面的方法来计算偏导数

$$
\frac {\partial u}{\partial x _ {1}} = - \frac {F _ {x _ {1}}}{F _ {u}}, \frac {\partial u}{\partial x _ {2}} = - \frac {F _ {x _ {2}}}{F _ {u}}, \dots , \frac {\partial u}{\partial x _ {n}} = - \frac {F _ {x _ {n}}}{F _ {u}},\tag{6.54b}
$$

但是此处将会用到 (6.52b)．按同样的方法可计算高阶导数．

(3) 若两个一元函数 $y = f \left( x \right) , z = \varphi \left( x \right)$ 由

$$
F (x, y, z) = 0 \quad {\text {和}} \quad \varPhi (x, y, z) = 0\tag{6.55a}
$$

构成的方程组给出．根据 (6.51b), (6.55a) 的微分为

$$
F _ {x} + F _ {y} y ^ {\prime} + F _ {z} z ^ {\prime} = 0, \quad \varPhi_ {x} + \varPhi_ {y} y ^ {\prime} + \varPhi_ {z} z ^ {\prime} = 0,\tag{6.55b}
$$

故

$$
y ^ {\prime} = \frac {F _ {z} \varPhi_ {x} - \varPhi_ {z} F _ {x}}{F _ {y} \varPhi_ {z} - F _ {z} \varPhi_ {y}}, \quad z ^ {\prime} = \frac {F _ {x} \varPhi_ {y} - F _ {y} \varPhi_ {x}}{F _ {y} \varPhi_ {z} - F _ {z} \varPhi_ {y}}.\tag{6.55c}
$$

由 $y ^ { \prime } , z ^ { \prime }$ ，利用 (6.55b) 的微分，用同样的方法可计算二阶导数 $y ^ { \prime \prime }$ 和 $z ^ { \prime \prime }$

(4) 个一元函数令 $y _ { 1 } = f \left( x \right) , y _ { 2 } = \varphi \left( x \right) , \cdot \cdot \cdot , y _ { n } = \psi \left( x \right)$ 个方程

$$
F (x, y _ {1}, y _ {2}, \dots , y _ {n}) = 0, \quad \varPhi (x, y _ {1}, y _ {2}, \dots , y _ {n}) = 0, \dots , \varPsi (x, y _ {1}, y _ {2}, \dots , y _ {n}) = 0\tag{6.56a}
$$

构成的方程组给出．由 (6.51b) ，可得 (6.56a) 的微分

$$
\left. \begin{array}{c} F _ {x} + F _ {y _ {1}} y _ {1} ^ {\prime} + F _ {y _ {2}} y _ {2} ^ {\prime} + \dots + F _ {y _ {n}} y _ {n} ^ {\prime} = 0, \\ \varPhi_ {x} + \varPhi_ {y _ {1}} y _ {1} ^ {\prime} + \varPhi_ {y _ {2}} y _ {2} ^ {\prime} + \dots + \varPhi_ {y _ {n}} y _ {n} ^ {\prime} = 0, \\ \vdots + \quad \vdots + \quad \vdots + \quad \vdots + \quad \vdots = 0, \\ \varPsi_ {x} + \varPsi_ {y _ {1}} y _ {1} ^ {\prime} + \varPsi_ {y _ {2}} y _ {2} ^ {\prime} + \dots + \varPsi_ {y _ {n}} y _ {n} ^ {\prime} = 0. \end{array} \right\}.\tag{6.56b}
$$

(6.56b)，可得要求的导数 $y _ { 1 } ^ { \prime } , y _ { 2 } ^ { \prime } , \cdots , y _ { n } ^ { \prime }$ 用类似的方法可计算高阶导数．

(5) 若两个二元函数 $u = f \left( x , y \right) , v = \varphi \left( x , y \right)$ 由

$$
F (x, y, u, v) = 0 \quad {\text {和}} \quad \varPhi (x, y, u, v) = 0\tag{6.57a}
$$

构成的方程组给出．利用 (6.52b), (6.57a) 的关千 $x , y$ 的微分为

$$
\left. \begin{array}{l} \frac {\partial F}{\partial x} + \frac {\partial F}{\partial u} \frac {\partial u}{\partial x} + \frac {\partial F}{\partial v} \frac {\partial v}{\partial x} = 0, \\ \frac {\partial \varPhi}{\partial x} + \frac {\partial \varPhi}{\partial u} \frac {\partial u}{\partial x} + \frac {\partial \varPhi}{\partial v} \frac {\partial v}{\partial x} = 0. \end{array} \right\}\tag{6.57b}
$$

$$
\left. \begin{array}{l} \frac {\partial F}{\partial y} + \frac {\partial F}{\partial u} \frac {\partial u}{\partial y} + \frac {\partial F}{\partial v} \frac {\partial v}{\partial y} = 0, \\ \frac {\partial \varPhi}{\partial y} + \frac {\partial \varPhi}{\partial u} \frac {\partial u}{\partial y} + \frac {\partial \varPhi}{\partial v} \frac {\partial v}{\partial y} = 0. \end{array} \right\}\tag{6.57c}
$$

分别从方程组 (6.57b) (6.57c) 中解出 $\frac { \partial u } { \partial x } , \frac { \partial v } { \partial x }$ 和 ${ \frac { \partial u } { \partial y } } , { \frac { \partial v } { \partial y } }$ 即得一阶偏导数利用同样的方法可计算高阶偏导数．

(6) 个方程构成的方程组给出的 元函数 利用与前面类似的方法可计算一阶和高阶偏导数
